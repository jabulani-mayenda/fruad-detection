Findings

Key Takeaways

🎯
Random Forest is the superior model for fraud detection
With 94.2% precision and 91.8% recall, it dramatically outperforms Logistic Regression while maintaining 99.9% overall accuracy on the highly imbalanced test set.
⚖️
SMOTE significantly improved model performance
Applying SMOTE only to training data (not test) doubled the F1 score for both models compared to training on raw imbalanced data — validating the preprocessing approach.
📊
V17, V14, V12 are the strongest fraud indicators
These PCA-transformed features consistently appeared at the top of Random Forest feature importance rankings, suggesting they encode the most discriminative transaction patterns.
⚡
Logistic Regression remains valuable as an explainable baseline
Despite lower precision (71.3%), its high recall (88.2%) and interpretability make it suitable for risk-averse environments where model transparency is legally required.
🔍
AUC-ROC is the right evaluation metric for this problem
With only 0.17% fraud rate, accuracy alone is misleading. AUC-ROC captures performance across all decision thresholds — critical for fine-tuning the precision-recall trade-off in production systems.
